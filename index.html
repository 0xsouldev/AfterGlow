Getting Apple Music in

Afterglow listens to whatever you share and draws it. Audio and camera frames stay on your device. Optional lyric searches send the song title and artist to LRCLIB.

  1. WEB
    Apple Music in a browser tabPress Tab or system, choose the music.apple.com tab and keep the tab-audio switch on in the picker.
  2. WIN
    Apple Music app on WindowsPress Tab or system, choose Entire screen and switch on system audio. Works in Chrome and Edge.
  3. MAC
    On a MacShare the Apple Music browser tab. Whole-system audio may not be offered on macOS.
  4. FILE
    Your own audio filesPress File or drop an MP3, M4A, WAV or FLAC anywhere. Downloaded Apple Music tracks are copy-protected, so use capture for those.

Inside an embedded preview, tab, system and mic capture are blocked. Open the standalone Afterglow file in Chrome or Edge for live capture. Files and the demo beat work here.
